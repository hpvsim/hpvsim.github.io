title: HPV DNA screening and vaccination strategies in Tunisia
status: peer-reviewed
order: 3
lead: >-
  A cost-effectiveness analysis comparing HPV DNA-based cervical screening
  strategies against the existing Pap-smear approach, both layered on top of
  Tunisia's 2025 school-based HPV vaccination program for girls aged 11-12.
researchThemes: [vaccination-access, screening-treatment]
themes: [Screening, HPV DNA testing]
setting: Tunisia
findings:
  - >-
    All screening strategies combined with vaccination substantially reduced
    cervical cancer cases, with cumulative-case decreases of 41% to 59%
    between 2025 and 2090.
  - >-
    The most intensive strategy (HPV DNA testing every 5 years for women
    aged 35-60) achieved the largest reduction but also the highest cost;
    maintaining current Pap-smear screening alongside vaccination was the
    most cost-effective option at today's test prices.
  - >-
    If the HPV DNA test price fell to $9, the most frequent testing strategy
    would become the most cost-effective option, offering both the greatest
    health benefit and financial viability.
