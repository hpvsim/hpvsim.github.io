lead: >-
  Evaluating the impact of a range of potential HPV therapeutic vaccines with varying scale-up of existing preventive interventions in high-burden low- and middle-income countries (LMICs).
researchThemes: [screening-treatment]
themes: [Therapeutic vaccines]
findings:
  - >-
    A therapeutic vaccine clearing 90% of virus and regressing 50% of
    high-grade lesions, reaching 70% of women aged 35-45 from 2030, could
    avert 1.2-2.2 million cervical cancer cases across nine high-burden
    LMICs over 30 years.
  - >-
    Impact translates to 500,000-1.2 million cervical cancer deaths and
    20-40 million DALYs averted.
  - >-
    Impact is highly sensitive to vaccine characteristics, especially how
    long immune memory lasts, and to the pace of background screening and
    preventive vaccination scale-up.
