  Evaluates a model-based case for an infant HPV prophylactic vaccination
  program in Nigeria, alongside the standard adolescent schedule.
researchThemes: [vaccination-access]

setting: Nigeria
findings:
  - >-
    For vaccine-targetable cohorts, infant delivery may have potential to
    circumvent the difficulties with vaccinating out-of-school girls,
    depending on effective vaccine efficacy. Empirical data on infant HPV
    vaccine durability, immunogenicity, and delivery coverage through the
    routine childhood series are the missing pieces that would enable a
    policy decision.
