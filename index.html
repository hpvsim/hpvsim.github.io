HPVsim

HPVsim is an agent-based model of HPV transmission and progression through cervical disease to cancer. It supports country-specific vital dynamics, structured sexual networks, co-transmitting HPV genotypes, B- and T-cell mediated immunity, and high-resolution disease natural history, plus built-in tools for vaccination, screening, and treatment interventions. It is implemented in pure Python, extensively tested and documented, and runs in seconds to minutes on a laptop.

v3.0 (released July 2026) is built on the Starsim architecture and is the actively developed line. The pre-Starsim v2.3 release (April 2026) is frozen aside from critical bugfixes.

Installation pip install hpvsim

The HPVsim model has been applied to answer questions that broadly fall into four main research themes.

The role of single-dose HPV vaccination in expanding access

Sixteen country-specific HPVsim models quantify how much single-dose HPV vaccination schedules — endorsed by WHO in 2022 — have already expanded access across Gavi-supported countries facing vaccine supply constraints.
